# Design Context — Headliner

## Product
Headliner is a tour-routing agent. Give it an artist or comedian; it reads Qloo's taste graph
(heatmaps, audience overlap, local taste tags) and returns a routed tour: cities, venue sizes,
opening acts, a per-city marketing angle, and drafted booking pitches.

## Audience
- Independent touring musicians and comedians (and their managers) planning a run
- Talent buyers / bookers evaluating whether an act can sell in a market
- Hackathon judges from live entertainment (Live Nation), sports/media, and comedy

## Jobs to be done
1. "Where are my fans, really?" — see demand on a map before committing money
2. "Which cities, in what order, at what size?" — a route that doesn't bleed money
3. "Who should open?" — support acts whose audience overlaps without cannibalizing
4. "How do I pitch this room?" — a booking email with evidence

## Personality
Tour poster / backstage tour book. Editorial, confident, tactile, a little loud.
Three words: **inked, routed, sold-out.**
It should feel like something printed for the road crew, not a SaaS dashboard.

## Visual direction
- Warm uncoated-paper background, near-black ink, one hot spot color (poster red-orange)
  plus a secondary halftone blue used sparingly for audience-overlap data.
- Display: heavy condensed grotesk in all caps (gig-poster headlines, set lists).
  Body: a readable serif or humanist sans with character — never Inter/Roboto/system default.
  Numbers: tabular figures (dates, capacities, scores).
- Rules, ticket stubs, numbered set-list rows, stamped labels. Hairline rules over boxes and cards.
- Map styled as print: muted land, ink route line that draws itself, red-orange heat.

## Motion
Critically damped springs by default; the one big moment is the route drawing itself city by city.
Respect prefers-reduced-motion (cross-fade, no draw animation).

## Anti-references
Purple/blue gradients, glowing dark dashboards, glassmorphism everywhere, identical card grids,
hero-metric templates, emoji icons.
